🟠 Phase 1 · Weeks 1–3

Zapier — your starting point

Zapier is the easiest automation tool to learn. It has a visual drag-and-drop interface and connects to all your Google Workspace apps out of the box. You will build your confidence here before moving to n8n.

1

Zapier Official "From A to Zap" Free

A three-course program made by Zapier itself — always up to date. Covers automating your work, building your first Zap step-by-step, and customizing with multi-step workflows. Completable in under an hour. Start here. Do this first.

Why it fits you: The course uses Gmail and Google Sheets as its primary examples throughout — exactly the tools you use every day.
learn.zapier.com
2

Full Zapier Tutorial for Beginners 2024 — The Social Guide Free · YouTube

A comprehensive video guide designed for people completely new to automation. It reinforces the official course with a different teaching style — more visual and narrative. Watch it alongside your Zapier account: pause, build, continue.

Why it fits you: Seeing the same concepts explained a second way is one of the fastest paths to true understanding.
classcentral.com
⚠️
About Zapier's free plan: The free tier allows 5 Zaps and 100 tasks per month — enough to learn and practice. Focus your practice automations on Google Workspace connections (Gmail, Sheets, Drive, Forms) to stay within limits while building the skills most relevant to your job.

✅ Your Week 1–3 Challenges

A
Form → Email: When a Google Form is submitted → automatically send a Gmail notification to yourself confirming the submission.
B
Sheet → Email: When a new row is added to a Google Sheet → automatically send a Gmail summary with the row's content.
C
Drive → Sheet log: When a new file is added to a Google Drive folder → automatically log the file name and date in a Google Sheet.
★
Bonus — your own job: Find one repetitive task in your daily work and automate it using what you have learned. This is the most important step of Phase 1.
🔵 Phase 2 · Weeks 4–6

n8n — next level power

n8n is an open-source automation tool that is far more powerful than Zapier. It handles complex logic, conditions, filters, and even AI workflows. You are ready for this once you have built real Zaps in Phase 1 — do not skip Phase 1.

3

Master n8n in 2 Hours — Jono Catliff Free · YouTube

One of the most complete free resources for n8n beginners. Covers the interface, triggers, actions, variable mapping, filters, switches, merge operations, webhooks, HTTP requests, and Google Drive integration — including how to build a full AI agent.

Why it fits you: The Google Drive integration section directly applies to your stack. Search "Master n8n in 2 Hours Jono Catliff" on YouTube.
classcentral.com — n8n guide
4

n8n Community Video Course Free · 50+ Videos

An actively maintained playlist built by the n8n community, updated weekly. It is designed to be the best n8n course for all levels — from beginner to advanced. Use this as your ongoing reference library after Jono Catliff's guide is done.

Why it fits you: New videos drop weekly so it always covers the latest n8n features. Bookmark this and return to it regularly.
community.n8n.io
🛠️
Getting started with n8n: Sign up for the free cloud trial at n8n.io. No credit card required. This gives you a working n8n environment in your browser — no installation needed to start learning.

✅ Your Week 4–6 Challenges

A
Rebuild in n8n: Take one automation you already built in Zapier and recreate it in n8n. Notice the differences. n8n gives you more control at every step.
B
Gmail → Sheets with filtering: Only log emails that contain a specific keyword in the subject line — something Zapier's free tier cannot easily do.
C
Multi-step Google workflow: A Google Form submission triggers three things at once: log to Sheet, send Gmail confirmation, and create a Drive folder.
★
Your own original workflow: Build one automation from scratch that saves you real time every week. This is your graduation project.

💡 Tips for Success

How to get the most from this course

🏗️

Always build, never just watch

Pause the video after each concept and recreate what you just saw in your own account. This is non-negotiable.

🎯

Use real tasks from your job

As early as Week 3, automate something you actually do every day. Personal relevance makes everything stick faster.

🐢

Do not rush to n8n

n8n has a steeper learning curve. Employees who skip Phase 1 often struggle. Finish all three Zapier weeks first.

🔍

When you get stuck, search n8n community

The n8n community forum is extremely active. Almost every question you will have has already been answered there.

📝

Document your workflows

Add notes and descriptions to every workflow you build. Future-you will thank you — and so will your colleagues.

🤝

Share what you build

Show your automations to your team. Teaching someone else is the fastest way to deepen your own understanding.
